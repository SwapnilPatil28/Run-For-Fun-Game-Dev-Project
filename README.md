# Run For Fun 🎮🏰✨

**Run For Fun** is a classic-inspired arcade platformer where you play as a brave knight on a lighthearted adventure!  

Either you **SACRIFICE** the shiny coins and try to win the game, or take the bold decision to collect all the coins and maybe try not to sacrifice yourself. That's a **Challenge!!!**  

Jump, run, and dodge through tricky obstacles as you avoid small slime monsters, leap across platforms, and collect shiny coins. But beware—if you fall off the land or touch an enemy, the challenge resets, pushing you to try again and master your timing.

This game was created for educational purposes and built using some assets made freely available by Brackeys, customized into a complete playable experience.

If you enjoy short, fun, and classic platformer challenges, **Run For Fun** is made for you!

---

## 🎮 Features

- Retro arcade feel with simple, fun mechanics  
- Smooth platforming action—move, jump, and dodge with precision  
- Collect coins and chase your high score  
- Quick restarts to keep the action flowing  

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

- **Controls:** Arrow keys / WASD to move and jump  
- **Goal:** Avoid enemies, jump across platforms, and collect coins  
- **Restart:** Fall or touch an enemy to try again
